Our scents

With scents like Ocean Breeze and Pine and Oud Woody, you'll feel both fresh and masculine all day long. Subtly understated, our scents work as well in the boardroom as on a date.

Scent one

Ocean Breeze & Pine

Our Ocean Breeze and Pine scent starts with a fresh and invigorating feeling, similar to what you experience by the sea. The scent then moves more towards wood and spice.

Top
Green, pine/eucalyptus
Heart
Cumin, mint, marine and pine
Base
Wood, balsam fir, aromatic

Scent two

Oud Woody

Oud Woody begins with oud notes that lean slightly smoky and leathery with a light sweetness. This gives the scent a mysterious and seductive dimension, followed by woody notes such as cedar, sandalwood or patchouli.

Top
Pepper, cardamom and olibanum
Heart
Guaiac, aquilaria, saffron
Base
Leather, ambergris, citrus
