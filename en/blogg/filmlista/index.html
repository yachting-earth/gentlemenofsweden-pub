A gentleman's movie list

A while ago we asked our gentlemen what their favorite movie was, unsurprisingly there were many classics in all genres but also the occasional bubbler. The drama category won big with 33.3% against action which reached 19.4% and Science Fiction in third place with 8.3%.

Now, this is obviously not a scientific study and certainly not an entirely accurate classification of the genre to which the films belong, but regardless, it gives a small indication of what our gentlemen watch when the days are short and the weather shows its worse side.

Anyway, I found some suggestions for movies that I both want to see and see again and with a little luck you did too.
