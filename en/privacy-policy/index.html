Privacy policy

Here you can find a shorter version of our privacy policy

Personal data

Gentlemen of Sweden AB processes the personal data you provide to us. Your data is never sold to third parties. It is only used to:

  • Answer your question or message to us
  • Communicate with you digitally, for example via email
  • Keep statistics and improve the website

What data we collect

When you contact us via a form on the website (for example the contact form or the distributor form), we may handle:

  • Contact details (name, email, phone number if provided)
  • The content of your message to us

In addition, we collect anonymized statistics about visits to the website via Google Analytics.

Who we share your data with

For statistics and to improve the website, we share anonymized or aggregated visit data with:

  • Google Analytics

We do not share your personal data with any other third parties.

How long we keep your data

  • Messages sent via our contact forms go directly as email to us (mailto) — we do not keep our own database of the information from the form; it is handled as regular email correspondence.
  • Visit statistics in Google Analytics are stored according to Google's standard retention settings.

Cookies

We use Google Analytics to keep statistics on visits and improve the website, which sets cookies in your browser. The website has no user accounts, no shopping cart and no comment functions, so we do not use cookies for login or similar purposes.

Your rights

As a visitor, you can:

  • Request a summary of the personal data we hold about you
  • Request that we delete your personal data, except what we are legally required to keep (for example accounting records)
  • Have incorrect data corrected
  • Withdraw your consent to data processing

For help, contact the data controller: gdpr@gentlemenofsweden.se
