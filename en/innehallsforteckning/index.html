Ingredients

Here you'll find short descriptions of the raw materials Gentlemen of Sweden uses in its products. All raw materials are sourced from Sweden or Europe.

Aqua

Water – the basic foundation of cosmetic products, providing moisture and helping to blend ingredients.

Argania Spinosa Kernel Oil

Argan oil – used to moisturise and nourish hair and skin, rich in antioxidants.

Ascorbyl Palmitate

A vitamin C derivative with antioxidant properties that help protect the skin from damage.

Benzyl Alcohol, Benzyl Benzoate, Benzoic Acid

Preservatives and fragrance components in cosmetic products.

Betula Alba Bark/Leaf Extract, Betula Alba Juice

Extract from birch bark/leaves and birch juice, with potentially soothing and cleansing properties.

BH

Short for "Butylated Hydroxytoluene," an antioxidant that prevents oxidation and extends the shelf life of products.

Butylene Glycol

A humectant that improves product texture and the skin's moisture retention.

Butyrospermum Parkii Butter

Shea butter – rich in fatty acids and vitamins, used to moisturise and soften the skin.

Caprylic/Capric Triglyceride

A light fat derived from coconut oil, providing moisture and suppleness to the skin.

Cera Microcristallina

Microcrystalline wax – gives cosmetic products body and structure.

Ceteareth-20, Ceteareth-25, Cetearyl Alcohol

Emulsifiers and emollients that help blend oils and water.

Citric Acid

An alpha hydroxy acid that adjusts pH and can exfoliate the skin.

Cocamidopropyl Betaine

A cleansing agent that provides foam and cleansing in products such as shampoo and shower gel.

Coco-Glucoside

A mild surfactant that removes dirt and oil from the skin.

Coumarin

A natural aromatic compound that gives fragrance to cosmetic products.

Dehydroacetic Acid

A preservative that prevents bacterial growth in products.

Disodium Laureth Sulfosuccinate

A mild cleansing agent that removes dirt and oil from skin and hair.

Ethylhexylglycerin

A multifunctional ingredient that acts as an emollient and boosts the effect of preservatives. Also helps to moisturise the skin.

Eucalyptus Globulus Leaf Oil

Eucalyptus oil – used for its refreshing and soothing properties in skin care.

Glycerin

A humectant that keeps the skin hydrated.

Glycol Distearate

An ingredient used to give products a pearlescent shimmer. It also acts as an emollient and improves texture.

Guar Hydroxypropyltrimonium Chloride

A plant-based ingredient used as a conditioning agent to soften and detangle hair. Helps reduce static and improve manageability.

Hamamelis Virginiana Leaf Extract

Witch hazel – used for its astringent and soothing properties for the skin.

Helianthus Annuus Seed Extract, Helianthus Annuus Seed Cera

Sunflower seed extract and wax with potential antioxidant benefits.

Hydrogenated Microcrystalline Cera

A wax-like ingredient that gives body and structure to products such as lip balm and creams.

Hydrogenated Palm Glycerides Citrate

An emollient and moisturising ingredient in skin care products.

Hydrogenated Vegetable Oil

A vegetable oil used to soften and moisturise the skin.

Hydrolyzed Rice Protein

Rice protein that strengthens hair and adds volume and shine.

Hydrolyzed Wheat Protein

Wheat protein that can strengthen and moisturise both hair and skin.

Hydrolyzed Wheat Starch

Wheat starch with moisturising properties for the skin.

Humulus Lupulus Extract

Hop extract with potentially soothing and antioxidant properties.

Kaolin

A clay used in cleansing products and face masks for its absorbent and purifying properties.

Lecithin

A natural emulsifier that helps blend oils and water in cosmetics.

Limonene

A natural fragrance ingredient that gives products a citrus scent.

Linalool

An aromatic compound with a floral scent, used to fragrance products.

Menthol

A cooling ingredient that gives a refreshing sensation, used in products such as conditioner and creams.

Panthenol

Provitamin B5 that moisturises and improves the condition of skin and hair.

Paraffinum Liquidum

Mineral oil that moisturises and creates a protective barrier on the skin.

PEG-100 Stearate

An emulsifier that helps blend oils and water in skin care products.

PEG-14M

An emollient and emulsifier that gives products flexibility and stability.

PEG-40 Hydrogenated Castor Oil

An emulsifier and solubiliser used to blend ingredients.

Parfum (Fragrance)

A composition of natural or synthetic fragrance ingredients that gives the product a pleasant scent. The exact ingredients vary depending on the formula.

Petrolatum

A wax-like substance that protects and moisturises the skin by forming a barrier.

Phenoxyethanol

A preservative that prevents bacterial growth in products.

Polyglyceryl-3 Polyricinoleate

An emulsifier that blends oils and water in cosmetic formulas.

Potassium

A mineral used as a stabiliser and to balance the pH of cosmetic products.

Povidon (polyvinylpyrrolidon PVP, E 1201)

A water-soluble polyvinyl polymer used as a stabiliser. The one Gentlemen of Sweden uses is synthetically produced.

Sodium Chloride

Table salt, sometimes used in scrub products for its exfoliating properties.

Sodium Lauryl Sulfoacetate

A mild cleansing agent that creates foam and cleans effectively.

Sodium Methyl Cocoyl Taurate

A mild cleansing agent that removes dirt and oil without drying out the skin.

Sodium PCA

A moisturising ingredient that helps maintain the skin's moisture balance.

Sorbate

An acid used as a preservative to extend the shelf life of products.

Sorbic Acid

A naturally derived preservative that prevents microbial growth in products.

Stearic Acid

A fatty acid that gives stability and texture to cosmetic products.

Stearyl Stearate

A fatty acid that acts as an emollient and contributes to product texture.

Tocopherol

Vitamin E, an antioxidant that protects the skin from damage caused by free radicals.

Tocopheryl Acetate

A stable form of vitamin E, used for its antioxidant and moisturising properties.

Urtica Dioica Leaf Extract

Nettle extract with potentially soothing and purifying properties for the skin.

VP/VA Copolymer

A polymer that provides hold and fixation in hair care products.

Swedish hair and beard care. Vegan, locally produced and approved under the Green Salon certification.
