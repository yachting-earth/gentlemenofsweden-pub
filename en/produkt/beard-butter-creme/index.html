Beard Butter Cream

100 ML · OUD WOODY

Softens the beard and the skin beneath.

259 kr Recommended retail price

Gentlemen of Sweden Beard Butter Creme is a luxurious, easily absorbed and moisturizing beard cream enriched with both shea butter and sunflower seed wax. The cream gives your beard a lightweight firmness and stylish matte finish. Thanks to the shea butter and sunflower seed wax we use, Beard Butter Cream is also nourishing for your beard, keeping it fresh and healthy.

Of course, the cream also contains witch hazel and nettle extracts, which are known for their soothing properties on the skin; all for the good of your beard and beard base.

By using the beard cream every day, you give your beard a chance to become healthier with reduced skin problems on the scalp.

How to use

Apply to towel-dried beard and beard scalp for a smoothing and conditioning effect. For control and conditioning, apply to dry beard and beard scalp.
