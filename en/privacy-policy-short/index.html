Privacy policy – short version

At Gentlemen of Sweden AB, we care about your privacy. Here's the most important thing you need to know:

Want to read more details about how we handle your data? Read our full privacy policy here

1. We collect

  • Contact details: name, email, phone number if provided (when you get in touch via a form)
  • The content of your message to us

2. We use your data for

  • Answering your question or message
  • Statistics and improving the website (Google Analytics)

3. We share data with

  • Statistics: Google Analytics

4. How long do we keep your data?

  • Contact forms: sent directly as email to us — we keep no database of your information
  • Visit statistics (Google Analytics): according to Google's standard settings

5. Cookies and tracking

We use Google Analytics to keep statistics and improve the website. No accounts, no shopping cart, no comments — so no cookies for that.

6. Your rights

You can always:

  • See what data we hold about you
  • Have incorrect data corrected
  • Request that we delete your data (except what we are legally required to keep)
  • Withdraw your consent to data processing

For questions or help, contact us at gdpr@gentlemenofsweden.se
